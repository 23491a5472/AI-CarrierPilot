Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how CareerPilot AI collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our application and related services.

Last updated: September 11, 2026
Your privacy matters to us.

CareerPilot AI is an AI-powered career assistant designed to help users analyze resumes, understand job requirements, evaluate job compatibility and improve their job application materials.

This policy describes the types of information that may be processed when you use CareerPilot AI and how that information is used.

1 Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, CareerPilot AI may collect or process the following categories of information.

Account Information
Information associated with your CareerPilot account, such as authentication information and account identifiers.
Resume Information
Resume content that you upload, paste or otherwise provide for analysis.
Job Information
Job descriptions, job titles, requirements and related information that you provide.
AI-Generated Results
ATS analysis, job-match results, resume suggestions, cover letters and other generated content.
Usage Information
Information about how you interact with the application and its features.
Technical Information
Certain technical information may be processed by third-party services used by the application.

2 How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Provide and operate CareerPilot AI.
  • Analyze resumes and job descriptions.
  • Calculate ATS Resume Health scores.
  • Calculate Job Match scores.
  • Identify job requirements and relevant skills.
  • Provide resume improvement recommendations.
  • Generate cover letters and other career content.
  • Save your analyses and application-related information.
  • Improve application functionality and reliability.
  • Detect, prevent and address security or technical issues.
  • Display advertising where applicable.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.

3 Resume & Job Description Data

CareerPilot AI processes resume and job-description information that you voluntarily provide when using features such as ATS analysis and Job Match.

Resume information may include employment history, education, professional experience, skills, certifications and other information contained in the resume you submit.

Job information may include job titles, responsibilities, qualifications, required skills and other information contained in a job description.

Please review your information before submitting it.
Do not upload information that you do not have the right or permission to provide.

4 AI Processing

CareerPilot AI uses artificial intelligence technologies to provide features including resume analysis, semantic job matching, resume improvement and cover-letter generation.

Information submitted for AI-powered features may be processed through our backend systems and applicable AI service providers in order to generate the requested results.

CareerPilot's AI analysis is intended to provide assistance and recommendations. AI-generated results may not always be accurate or complete and should be reviewed by the user.

5 Data Storage

CareerPilot AI uses cloud-based infrastructure and database services to provide application functionality.

Depending on the features you use, information may be stored in our application database, including account information, resumes, job descriptions, analyses and generated content.

Data protection
We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

6 Third-Party Services

CareerPilot AI may use trusted third-party services to provide authentication, database infrastructure, AI processing, advertising and application functionality.

Supabase

CareerPilot AI uses Supabase for backend infrastructure, authentication and database services.

Google Gemini / Google AI

CareerPilot AI may use Google Gemini and related Google AI services to provide AI-powered analysis and content generation.

Google AdMob

CareerPilot AI may use Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may process certain device, advertising and usage information in accordance with Google's applicable policies and your advertising settings.

Third-party services may have their own privacy policies governing their processing of information.

7 Advertising

CareerPilot AI may display advertisements through third-party advertising providers such as Google AdMob.

Advertising providers may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, application interaction and other permitted information to deliver, measure and improve advertising.

You may be able to control certain advertising preferences through your device settings and applicable Google services.

8 Security

We take reasonable measures designed to protect information processed through CareerPilot AI.

These measures may include access controls, secure authentication mechanisms and appropriate cloud-security practices provided by our infrastructure providers.

Important: No method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9 Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide CareerPilot AI, maintain your account, provide application history, comply with legal obligations and protect our services.

The retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the feature through which it was collected.

When information is no longer required for legitimate purposes, it may be deleted or anonymized in accordance with our applicable data-management practices.

10 Account & Data Deletion

If you have a CareerPilot account, you may request deletion of your account and associated personal information through the account-management or deletion functionality provided by the application.

Where applicable, deletion requests may result in the removal of account information, resumes, analyses and other information associated with your account.

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary for security purposes or otherwise permitted by applicable law.

Important: The deletion process described here should match the actual account-deletion functionality implemented in the CareerPilot application.

11 Children's Privacy

CareerPilot AI is intended for individuals who are legally permitted to use career and employment-related services.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to CareerPilot AI inappropriately, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

12 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to CareerPilot AI, our services, technology, applicable laws or our data practices.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date displayed at the top of this page.
